# Media asset notes

## Homepage rooftop installation background

- `public/hero-rooftop-installers.webp`: AI-generated illustrative image of two Black male solar installers working on a residential rooftop. It is not a photograph of Solara employees or a verified Solara project.
- `public/hero-rooftop-installers-mobile.webp`: portrait crop of the same generated image, focused on the installer on the right for small screens.
- Both images are optimized WebP assets served locally. The original generation PNG is omitted to avoid shipping an unnecessary large duplicate.
- The slow pan-and-zoom effect is CSS animation of a still image, not video or animated physical installation. Visitors can pause it; reduced-motion preferences disable it automatically.
